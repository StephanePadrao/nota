A day in Hong Kong, from the Star Ferry to nightfall. Here you cross the city by boat: you hop from one ferry to the next and watch the skyline grow from the water.

By day, Victoria Harbour is blue and mineral. By night, a wall of neon reflected in the harbour, crossed by a red-sailed junk straight off a postcard.

In between: the back-streets, the bilingual signs, a fire station that goes by '消防局'. Shot on a phone.
