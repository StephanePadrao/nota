A week in Finistère to unwind, walk, and take the time to look.

The GR34 runs along cliffs that make you feel like you're at the edge of the world. The light in Brittany is peculiar — diffuse, never harsh, it shifts every twenty minutes.

What surprised me most: the silence. Even in August, some coastal trails are nearly deserted. Brittany isn't Instagram yet.
